# FAIR-D / CARE-D codebook — version 1.0

**Unit of analysis.** Public digital resource/project containing linguistic data in at least one Indigenous language spoken in Mexico.

**Documentary cut-off.** 20 August 2026.

## Coding rule
Each indicator is coded as **0 = not located in the reviewed public documentation; 1 = partial, indirect, or ambiguous evidence; 2 = explicit and verifiable public evidence**. N/A is reserved for cases where an indicator is conceptually inapplicable. Absence of public documentation must not be interpreted as absence of practice.

## FAIR-D indicators
- **F1 — Persistent or stable identifier.** DOI, Handle, citable release, or other persistent identifier.
- **F2 — Rich descriptive metadata.** Language/variety, size, authors, date, content, and other relevant attributes.
- **F3 — Discoverability.** Documented indexing or presence in repositories, catalogues, portals, or specialised search systems.
- **A1 — Public access route.** Consultation, download, API, or repository accessible through a standard web protocol.
- **A2 — Explicit access conditions.** Restrictions, authentication, terms, or access modality are documented.
- **I1 — Structured machine-readable format.** CSV, JSON, XML/TEI, CLDF, CoNLL-U, API, or another structured format.
- **I2 — Interoperable standards/identifiers.** ISO codes, Glottocodes, controlled vocabularies, annotation standards, or similar.
- **R1 — Clear reuse licence.** Explicit and accessible data licence.
- **R2 — Provenance, sources, and citation.** Sources, creators, construction process, and/or recommended citation documented.
- **R3 — Versioning/community standard.** Releases, version history, change control, or explicit adherence to community standards.

## CARE-D indicators
- **C1 — Stated collective benefit.**
- **C2 — Return or use oriented to speakers/communities.**
- **CA1 — Documented Indigenous participation.**
- **CA2 — Authority and capacity to control data.** Consent alone does not satisfy this indicator.
- **CA3 — Withdrawal, correction, or restriction mechanism.**
- **CR1 — Relationship and accountability.**
- **CR2 — Capacity building, compensation, or training.**
- **CR3 — Community languages/frameworks embedded in infrastructure.**
- **CE1 — Consent, ethics, privacy, and harm prevention.**
- **CE2 — Justice, attribution, and future use.**

## Descriptive indices
FAIR-D and CARE-D are independently normalised to 0–100 over applicable indicators. They are descriptive measures of **public documentary visibility**, not ethical certifications or compliance scores.
