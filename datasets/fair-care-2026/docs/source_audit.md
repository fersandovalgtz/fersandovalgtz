## Particularly robust audit finding
Bui et al. (2026) explicitly documents that annotators belonged to the participating communities, were compensated at hourly rates comparable to secondary-school teachers in their regions, agreed to the CC BY-NC licence, and retained ownership of the dataset. This supports CA1, CA2, CR2, and CE1 without inferring governance from participation alone.

## Interpretive rule
No matrix score should be presented as FAIR or CARE certification. The dataset records publicly observable documentation only.
